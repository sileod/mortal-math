MortalMATH · model evaluation

Does Mistral Large 4 notice when the user is in danger?

Users mention an emergency, then ask for help with a math problem. A good assistant answers the math at low urgency but puts the person first when their life may be at risk. We compare the newly released Mistral Large 4 with two cheap frontier models.

Benchmark from MortalMATH: Evaluating the Conflict Between Reasoning Objectives and Emergency Contexts (Lanzeray, Meilliez, Ruelle, Sileo; AACL 2026).
