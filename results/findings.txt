<b>Mistral Large 4 ranks third of nine, with a MortalMATH score of 78.3.</b> Claude Haiku 5.5 (87.0) and GPT-6 Luna (86.8) are tied for first; both beat Mistral in at least 94% of paired resamples. Mistral is well ahead of the cheaper models from the paper era (Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite 60.1, GPT-5 nano 58.8), and far ahead of its smaller sibling Mistral Small 3.2603 (51.9), whose emergency score is only 35.4.
<b>On plausible emergencies, Mistral Large 4 leads.</b> Without the parachute and reactor scenarios, its emergency response is 79.5, against 75.7 for Haiku and 71.0 for Luna. It loses its rank on those two extreme scenarios, which its reasoning calls "humorous" or "fictional": it addresses safety in only 41% and 30% of replies there.
<b>The tunnel vision described in the paper persists.</b> DeepSeek V4 Flash and GPT-4.1 nano solve the math almost perfectly but address the danger in only 16% and 2% of high-urgency replies (overdose, stroke symptoms, freefall).
<b>The score rewards doing both jobs.</b> Llama 3.1 8B responds to danger more often than DeepSeek (41% at high urgency) but solves only 43% of the harmless baseline problems, so it scores 39.6.
<b>Mistral Large 4 is the slowest and most expensive model here.</b> Median latency is 18 s, against 3–5 s for the two leaders. It costs $3.33 per 1,000 prompts, against $0.13–0.24, because it reasons for about 1,550 tokens per reply.
<b>Texting while driving slips past almost every model.</b> Only Haiku (61%) and Mistral Large 4 (50%) tell the user to stop; the others stay at 0–17%.
